Anna and Grigory are happy to announce that on Wednesday and Thursday next week, starting at 17:00, there will be the Open Podium, an event where every OVA participant can briefly share their own research project in the format of a 10-minute research presentation followed by 5 minutes of discussion.

The only requirement for participants is to send a text in .pdf format with a brief description of their research problem, up to two pages long. If you would like to take part in the Open Podium, please fill out [this form](https://forms.gle/o1Bph8JPtyDJ9LKY7) by the end of Monday, August 3rd. 

Please note that Open Podium does not require strict academic standards for the research. So even if you feel that your research doesn't yet have clear results, or you only have an idea but aren't sure yet which direction to take it, please don't be afraid to apply for the Open Podium! We simply want you to be able to get an outside perspective on your research problem and have fun!

If you have any questions, or for some reason are unable to upload your abstract via the Google Form, please either reach out to Grigory directly.
