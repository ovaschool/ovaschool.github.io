We are happy to announce that as of yesterday, we have a full OVA board!

- General Member 1: Berit Gehrke

- Syntax Member: Magdalena Lohninger

The two new board members (Daria and Andrew) were officially elected by all OVA members at yesterday's general assembly. Welcome on board!

During the general assembly, we also got a lot of constructive feedback for future OVA schools - thanks to everyone who contributed, we will do our best to integrate all your great ideas in the organizing process.
